
## Reports, retention, and elected officials

Reports and analytics accept validated `From date` and `To date` values. If entered in reverse order, the system normalizes the range. The range applies to blotter reports, document requests, and feedback counts, and is preserved in PDF, CSV, and Word exports. Export actions are audit-logged with the selected filters.

Resident self-registration has been removed. Staff continue to create resident portal accounts through the controlled resident-account workflow.

Staff and administrator accounts have no delete action. A resigned or former staff member must be set to `Archived (retained)`, which prevents sign-in while preserving the account, audit history, and identity record. The active administrator cannot archive its own current session account.

Elected officials are managed from **Elected officials**. Each retained profile contains position, full name, official address, term end, and contact number. Active profiles appear in the official sidebar; former profiles can be archived from the sidebar without deletion. Configure the records using the approved barangay information before deployment.
